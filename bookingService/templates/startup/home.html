About Cousins Car Care

Our team of highly qualified automobile engineers possesses specialized skill sets dedicated to providing an elevated client experience. As trusted partners for top tyre brands (Apollo, CEAT, JK, Bridgestone) and RCM products, we ensure your vehicle receives showroom perfection.

Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our detailing process, location, or scheduling? Find quick answers below.

1. How can I locate Cousins Service Station in Kodungallur, Thrissur?

Cousins Service Station is located Near Gouri Shankar Hospital, Pullirada, Kodungallur, Thrissur. It is easily accessible via all main transport routes for first-time visitors.

2. How often should I get my car washed or detailed?

If you want to maintain your car's paint finish and cabin fresh for years, you should get it professionally washed or detailed at least once every two weeks.

3. Does the car wash remove all types of stains?

It depends on how quickly you get your car washed. Bringing your vehicle in promptly prevents stubborn environmental stains from embedding into the paint coating or leather seats.

4. What are your hours of operation?

We are open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM (Sunday: Holiday). Booking your slot in advance on our website guarantees a dedicated bay and hassle-free service.

5. How long does it take to wash or detail the car?

The time taken depends on the type of stains, vehicle size, and the detailing services chosen. Reserving your slot online helps streamline work execution.
